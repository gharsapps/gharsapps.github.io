Huroof Garden

Children aged 3 to 7 learn the 28 Arabic letters of the Quran through play, with every letter spoken by a real Qaida teacher.

  • Pop the Sound, Trace It, and Huroof Highway's three car games
  • Letters unlock step by step, and every star grows a flower in the garden
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts
  • Collects no data and works fully offline
