Huroof Garden Privacy Policy

Ghars Apps · Effective November 16, 2026

In short: Huroof Garden does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information, from children or from adults. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no internet access. Your child's progress stays on your device.

About this policy

This policy explains how the Huroof Garden app (the "app"), made by Ghars Apps ("we", "us"), handles information on Android and iOS. Huroof Garden is an educational game that teaches children aged about 3 to 7 the letters of the Arabic alphabet used in the Quran. It applies only to this app.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect any personal or sensitive information, including names, email addresses, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising IDs. The app does not ask for any device permissions, and it cannot connect to the internet, so it cannot send information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To remember your child's progress between sessions, the app saves a small amount of game data in the app's private storage on your device:

  • stars earned, in total and for each game;
  • how many letters have been unlocked;
  • the age setting chosen by a parent;
  • the toy cars won in the car games and which one is selected.

This data contains no personal information and never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time with "Reset progress" in the parents' area of the app, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

If your device's own backup is turned on (for example Android backup or iCloud backup), your device may include this game data in your personal backup, under your account and that provider's privacy terms. We never receive or see it.

Speech

The app plays letter sounds recorded by a teacher and bundled with the app. If a recording is missing, the app may use your device's built-in text-to-speech to say a letter or word. That speech is produced by your device's operating system; the app does not record or listen to audio and does not use the microphone.

Sharing of information

We do not share, sell or rent any information, because we do not collect any. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or social media software, and no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Huroof Garden is made for young children. It is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and similar laws, and with the Google Play Families Policy, by not collecting any personal information from anyone. Links and settings meant for adults sit behind a parent gate.

App store data

When you download the app, Google Play or the Apple App Store may process information about you under their own privacy policies. We may see anonymous, aggregated statistics those stores provide to developers (such as the number of installs), which do not identify you or your child.

Security

Because the app collects and sends no personal information, there is no personal data held by us to protect. Game progress stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released. If we ever planned to collect personal information from children, we would first obtain verifiable parental consent as the law requires.
